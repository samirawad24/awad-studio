Settings

Recording delay fix
Every computer adds a small delay between hearing the beat and recording your voice. The studio moves your takes back by this amount so they land on the beat.
ms

Export song

The metronome is never included. Muted tracks are left out. Turn on Auto master in the Mixer for a louder, finished sound.

Starter beat

Pick a style. You get drums, an 808 and a melody in the song key, placed in the song for 8 bars. Change anything you want after.

Song layout

Marks the parts of a song on the ruler. Click a part name on the ruler to jump to it.

Chord helper

Pick a mood. Four chords in the song key go into this pattern for the selected instrument, one chord per bar.

Auto-tune

Pulls your singing onto the notes of the key. The tuned version is added as a new take, so you can switch back with the Take arrows.

Friend code

Pulling beats from YouTube needs the friend code. Ask whoever sent you this link.
